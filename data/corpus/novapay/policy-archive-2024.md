# Policy Archive (2024–2026) — SUPERSEDED

> ## ⚠ THIS DOCUMENT IS NO LONGER IN FORCE
>
> Every policy below has been **superseded**. It is retained only as a historical
> record, so that transactions and accounts governed by the older terms can be
> reasoned about correctly.
>
> **Do not quote this document as current policy.** For policy in force today see
> **Refunds and Disputes** and **Payout Schedules**.
>
> Each section states the exact window during which it applied.

This archive exists because NovaPay's obligations to a merchant are determined by
the policy in force at the time of the relevant event, not by the policy in force
when the question is asked. A refund on a 2025 transaction is governed by the 2025
window even if it is requested today.

---

## Refund window — 90 days

**In force: 15 January 2024 to 28 February 2026. Superseded 1 March 2026.**

> Refunds may be issued **within 90 days of the original transaction**. After 90
> days a refund cannot be issued through NovaPay, and merchants wishing to return
> funds must do so by other means.
>
> The window is measured from the date of the original transaction, not from the
> date the refund is requested.

**Current policy: 120 days.** The window was extended effective 1 March 2026. See
**Refunds and Disputes**.

**Which applies:** the window is determined by the date of the **original
transaction**.

| Original transaction date | Refund window |
|---|---|
| On or after 1 March 2026 | 120 days |
| Before 1 March 2026 | 90 days |

A transaction from January 2026 therefore has a 90-day window even though the
current published policy is 120 days.

## New-merchant payout hold — T+14 on the first three payouts

**In force: 1 June 2023 to 9 January 2026. Superseded 10 January 2026.**

> New accounts are subject to an extended payout hold. The **first three payouts**
> on a new account are held to **T+14 business days**. From the fourth payout
> onward the account settles on its ordinary schedule.
>
> The hold begins at account verification. Express payout is not available for a
> held payout.

**Current policy: T+7 business days, first payout only.** See **Payout Schedules**.

**Which applies:** accounts verified before 10 January 2026 completed their hold
under the previous terms and were not migrated. An account verified in
December 2025 served three T+14 holds; an account verified in February 2026 serves
one T+7 hold.

## Dispute representment window — 5 calendar days

**In force: 1 June 2023 to 28 February 2026. Superseded 1 March 2026.**

> Merchants have **5 calendar days** from dispute notification to submit evidence.

**Current policy: 7 calendar days.** See **Refunds and Disputes**.

---

## Starter plan volume limit — $25,000

**In force: 1 June 2023 to 31 January 2026. Superseded 1 February 2026.**

> Starter accounts may process up to **$25,000 per calendar month**.

**Current policy: $50,000 per calendar month.** See **Account Limits**.

Unlike the refund window and the payout hold, this change applied to **all existing
accounts immediately** on 1 February 2026. No account remained on the $25,000
ceiling after that date, so this section is of historical interest only and does not
govern any account today.

---

## Why some changes migrate and others do not

The pattern above is not arbitrary, and it is the source of most confusion about
this archive:

- **Changes that only benefit the merchant and carry no risk exposure** — the
  Starter volume increase — were applied to all accounts at once.
- **Changes tied to a specific transaction** — the refund window, the representment
  window — follow the transaction, because the terms were those in force when it
  was processed.
- **Changes tied to an account's onboarding risk assessment** — the payout hold —
  follow the account, because the hold was part of the terms the account was
  approved under.

When determining which applies, the question is always *what event is this policy
attached to?*, and then *what was in force when that event happened?*
